# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository, Discussion, and community space of the
Publisle organization.

Treat everyone with respect. Welcome questions, give constructive feedback, and
focus criticism on ideas and code. Respect people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private
information without consent are not welcome in this project's spaces.

To report a concern privately, email [the maintainers](mailto:coder64bit@gmail.com).
Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers will review concerns
discreetly, hear relevant perspectives, and respond proportionately. Responses
may include a warning, removing content, or restricting participation.
Maintainers follow these same expectations.
